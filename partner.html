✍️ Consent Agreement

Read and confirm the GeoEstate Platform Consent Agreement.

GEOESTATE PLATFORM CONSENT AGREEMENT — PARTNER / PROPERTY OWNER

By submitting this listing, I confirm and agree to the following:

1. Property Ownership: I confirm I am the lawful owner or authorized representative of the listed property, and that all title documents submitted are genuine.

2. Platform Fee: I agree to GeoEstate's 10% platform service fee, deducted upon successful deal close.

3. No Off-Platform Payments: I agree NOT to request, accept, or facilitate any payment outside the GeoEstate escrow system.

4. Accuracy: All property information, photos, and pricing are accurate. Misleading listings will be removed immediately.

5. Law Enforcement Cooperation: I consent to GeoEstate sharing my verified identity and transaction records with the NPF CID in the event of fraud or dispute.

6. Data Protection: I consent to GeoEstate storing my verification status and property records per the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
